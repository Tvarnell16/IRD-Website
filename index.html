FAQ

Answers to common questions

How long are lessons?

Most micro-lessons run 30–60 minutes with optional follow-up notes.

Can I swap skills instead of paying?

Yes. Teachers can choose to accept a skill swap, paid booking, or both.

What do I need to host a lesson?

You only need a clear lesson outline, a webcam, and a quiet space.

How do ratings work?

After each session, both learner and teacher leave feedback to build trust.
